# VPD Chart Card

Visualize Vapor Pressure Deficit (VPD) for optimal plant growth!

## Features

- 📊 Interactive VPD chart with color-coded zones
- 📈 24-hour history graph
- 🌱 Growth stage optimization
- 🍃 Leaf temperature support
- 🌡️ Fahrenheit & Celsius support
- ⚠️ Smart calibration alerts

Perfect for indoor gardening and grow rooms!
